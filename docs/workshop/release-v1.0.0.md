# NOM: Noise of Mist v1.0.0: the first release

After a long road of sprints, late-night playtests and a lot of fog, **NOM: Noise of Mist is
finally out.** This is the first official version, and I could not be happier to share it.

It started as a simple idea: what if the night in Project Zomboid felt like The Last of Us, and the
fog felt like Silent Hill? Version 1.0.0 is that idea, finished and polished, and it is playable in
solo and on dedicated servers.

## What you get

- **A night that hunts you.** Zombies get faster and see and hear farther. A lit flashlight
  outdoors is a beacon. The dark is darker and colder, and the bodies of those who died before dusk
  come back as **Echoes**.
- **The fog.** Sirens wail from every side, the zombies freeze, and 30 seconds later the fog rolls
  in. The world loses its color, the walls start to bleed and decay, and the monsters come out:
  - **Clickers**: blind, they hunt by sound. Their click is a sonar ring: crouch and stay still
    and it passes you by.
  - **Runners**: they scream when they see you and bring the horde.
  - **The Mourner**: she sobs in the fog. Don't wake her.
  - **The Faceless**: look at it and it vanishes, only to come back closer, behind you.
- **The red fog.** Lower, torn sirens: everything inside it is a monster.
- **The black fog.** An out-of-tune siren, darkness at noon, and every zombie becomes a
  **Cinder**, which only moves in the dark. Your light is all that holds them back, and your
  flashlight will stutter at the worst possible moment.
- **Storms.** Lightning and thunder roll through the red and black fogs (a flash freezes the
  Cinders for a heartbeat), street lamps flicker, and sometimes the rain comes with them.
- **Original 3D monster pieces**: the Clicker's barbed-wire blindfold, the Runner's torn mouth, the
  Mourner's hair, the Cinder's charcoal crust. All of them, and every sound and texture, are made
  by our own scripts.
- **Two optional mods in the same item:** a fog **Shader** (grain, chromatic aberration,
  distortion) and the experimental **Volumetric** fog, which flows around houses and cars and
  parts around you as you walk through it (needs ZombieBuddy).
- **Your rules.** A full sandbox page lets you tune every system: fog frequency and length, the red
  and black fog, night strength and each monster's chance.

## Good to know

- Build 42.20 or newer.
- Formerly "Névoa e Outro Mundo". The mod IDs are now `NoiseOfMist`, `NoiseOfMist_Shader` and
  `NoiseOfMist_Volumetrica`; saves started with the old IDs still ask for the old ones.
- Removing the mod from a running save does not break the save.
