[h1]Névoa e Outro Mundo (Fog and Otherworld)[/h1]
Horror for Build 42, inspired by The Last of Us and Silent Hill. [b]The night brings infected flesh. The fog brings the Otherworld.[/b]

[h2]Night[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every zombie gets faster and sees and hears farther. A lit flashlight outdoors is a beacon.
[*]Every so often, zombies near you come to you even without seeing you.
[*]The night is much darker and colder than vanilla. Light is what separates seeing from not seeing.
[*][b]Echoes[/b]: bodies of those who died before dusk release a weak soul, once per body. No corpse, no loot, gone at dawn. Burn or bury your dead.
[/list]

[h2]Fog[/h2]
Almost every day, at any hour, distant sirens wail from every side and the zombies stop where they stand. The fog starts rising and, 30 seconds later, rolls in for real, by day or by night, for 3 to 5 hours (the red one, 4 to 6): the world darkens and loses color, a low drone replaces the ambience, radios and TVs hiss on their own, decay takes floors and walls, the walls bleed, the screen edges close in and the picture turns to grainy film (Options > Mods: intensity). There is no other fog: the game's natural fog is gone. Every monster except the Echoes lives only in the fog:
[list]
[*][b]Clickers[/b]: blind, guided only by sound, clicking as a warning. Crouch, stay quiet and walk right past.
[*][b]Runners[/b]: sprinters that scream when they spot you and call the zombies around. They start the horde.
[/list]
[b]The Mourner[/b] stands still in the fog, sobbing quietly. Get close, point your flashlight at her or fire a shot nearby and she screams: every zombie for 50 tiles comes, and she runs straight at you.
[b]The Faceless[/b] walks in the fog. Look at it, or shine a light on it, and it vanishes, only to come back closer, behind you. Get too close and it attacks. Your head fills with radio static when one is near.
Sometimes the sirens sound lower and torn: that is the [b]red fog[/b]. Fog and light turn red and every zombie inside it is a monster.
Night and fog together: everything at once. The worst night possible, on purpose.

[h2]Sandbox[/h2]
Own page, "Fog and Otherworld": a toggle and numbers for every system (fog frequency and length, red fog, night strength, chance of each monster, Echoes, screen effects). "Light" and "Nightmare" presets on GitHub.

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
Works in solo and on dedicated servers, same code. The server decides night, fog, variants and Echoes; sound, vignette and ground stains are local to each player and never touch the save or the network. On a server, add it to Mods and WorkshopItems; clients download it automatically.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Build 42.20 or newer (older builds mark it as unavailable).
[*]Adds no items, recipes or map changes. Should play along with most mods; mods that also rewrite zombie speed and senses at night will fight over them.
[*]Removing it from a running save does not break the save.
[/list]

[h2]Optional mods in this item[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shader[/b]: chromatic aberration, grain and distortion in the fog. Not with ShadowZ; restart the game after turning it on or off.
[*][b]Volumetric[/b] (experimental): truly volumetric fog that flows around houses, trees and cars, rises against walls and parts around whoever walks through it. Visual only, client only. Needs [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url], set up as its page explains (a launch option); without it you get the regular fog.
[/list]
Fog atmosphere (sound, stains, screen) follows only the first player in split screen.
